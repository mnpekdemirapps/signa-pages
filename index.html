Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Why was my equation rejected?

A chain must follow connected tiles in sequence, use a supported expression, and satisfy the board's rule. The complete board may require a specific order even when another partial equation is mathematically true. If a valid chain appears to be rejected, send us the level number and a screenshot.

How do hints work?

A hint shows the next intended chain and its direction. You can earn or purchase additional hints in the shop. Rewarded ads may also grant one hint when available.

Will my progress sync to another device?

Signa does not currently use player accounts or cloud progress sync. Progress, settings, and hint balance are stored locally on your device and may be lost if the app is deleted.

Can I restore purchased hints?

Hint packs are consumable purchases; restore does not recreate consumed hint purchases. Unused hints have no time-based expiry while their local app data remains available. The non-consumable Pro purchase can be restored.

How do I report a problem?

Email mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com. Please include the level number, device model, operating system and app version, and a screenshot or short screen recording when possible.
